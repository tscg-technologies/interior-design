Modern Independent House Construction

Active structural development showing reinforced concrete columns, tower crane framework, and engineering supervision.

residential-construction.jpg

Project Overview & Civil Context

Complete architectural execution, RCC structural frame, brick masonry, plastering, premium flooring, plumbing, electrical conduit installation, and perimeter boundary wall construction.

Structural & Civil Works Executed

Foundation excavation, isolated footings, M25 grade RCC framing, fly-ash brick infill walls, waterproof terrace screed, vitrified tile flooring, and exterior anti-fungal weather-shield paint.

Construction Methodology & Engineering Standards

The project followed a standardized civil engineering matrix. Sub-soil bearing capacity was verified through standard penetration tests (SPT). Concrete mix designs incorporated controlled water-cement ratios with plasticizers to ensure high workability without strength loss. Mechanical needle vibrators were utilized across all column and beam pours to eliminate void cavities.

  • ✔ Earthquake Resistant: Design aligned with IS 1893 seismic criteria.
  • ✔ Anti-Termite Sub-slab Barrier: Chemical chlorpyrifos treatment executed prior to PCC pour.
  • ✔ Multi-layer Waterproofing: Elastomeric polymer membranes applied to sumps and balconies.

Project Execution Stages

Sequential Milestone Roadmap:

Site Survey & Excavation → Foundation & Footings → Plinth & Column Framework → Slab Casting → Masonry Works → Finishing & Handover
