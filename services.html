Civil Contracting Services

End-to-end contracting capabilities covering structural design execution, high-grade concrete framing, residential development, commercial builds, and arterial civil infrastructure.

Division 01

Residential Construction

Complete turnkey structural contracting for bespoke private dwellings, multi-tier duplex residences, private gated villas, and multi-unit residential apartment developments.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Individual House Construction: Custom foundation-to-parapet execution with soil-aligned footing design.
  • Duplex House Construction: Multi-level living spaces with internal cantilever staircases and high ceilings.
  • Villa Construction: Luxury expansive residences featuring integrated pools, verandas, and heavy structural framing.
  • Apartment Construction: Multi-storey RCC shear frame casting, lift cores, and underground sumps.
  • Residential Extensions: Adding vertical floors or lateral wings with structural load-bearing checks.

Division 02

Commercial Construction

Heavy-duty, functional civil engineering for enterprise workspaces, retail complexes, shopping destinations, multi-brand automotive showrooms, and commercial logistics hubs.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Office Buildings: Multi-tier column grids engineered for open floor plans and post-tensioned spans.
  • Commercial Complexes: Heavy footfall circulation structures, basement car parking, and service docks.
  • Retail Buildings: Multi-tenant retail floors with wide shopfront openings and reinforced lintels.
  • Showrooms: High-ceiling expansive floor slabs capable of supporting display vehicle loads.
  • Business Premises: Durable corporate headquarters built with energy-efficient thermal mass.

Division 03

Structural Works

The critical core of civil contracting: engineered reinforced cement concrete (RCC) works, deep foundation piling, monolithic casting, and seismic structural strengthening.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Foundation Works: Isolated footings, combined pads, deep raft slabs, and bored cast-in-situ piles.
  • RCC Works: Computerized mix designs utilizing high-yield strength Fe550D TMT reinforcement.
  • Column & Beam Construction: Tied rebar cages, high-precision formwork alignment, and mechanical vibration.
  • Slab Construction: Two-way slabs, ribbed slabs, flat plates, and waffle slab concrete casting.
  • Structural Repair & Strengthening: Rebar rust passivation, polymer mortar grouting, and carbon wrap.

Division 04

Civil Engineering Works

Mastering ground-level and subsurface civil challenges: massive site clearing, contour levelling, retention embankments, water holding reservoirs, and boundary enclosures.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Site Development: Clearing, topsoil scraping, grading, and establishing site datum levels.
  • Earthwork & Mass Excavation: Deep pit cutting, rock trenching, and soil backfill compaction.
  • Concrete Works: Plain cement concrete (PCC) mud mats, machine pedestals, and culvert aprons.
  • Drainage Works: Precast RCC box drains, underground soak pits, and perimeter retention swales.
  • Compound Walls: Precast boundary walls and traditional stone masonry retaining fences.

Division 05

Road & Infrastructure Works

Long-lasting vehicular roadways, industrial campus transit corridors, interlock paver walkways, and high-volume stormwater drainage systems engineered for heavy traffic loads.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Road Construction: Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) and asphaltic concrete road paving.
  • Pathways & Pedestrian Plazas: Heavy-duty interlocking concrete block pavers (ICBP) on sand bedding.
  • Paving Works: Vacuum Dewatered Concrete (VDF) flooring for parking lots and logistics yards.
  • Stormwater Drainage: Heavy discharge culverts, rainwater harvesting wells, and roadside channels.
  • Basic Infrastructure: Duct banks for power cabling, street light foundation casting, and sewer lines.

Division 06

Renovation & Remodeling

Breathing durable life into existing structures through precision structural intervention, load redistribution, commercial interior remodels, and moisture remediation.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Residential Renovation: Rebuilding internal walls, re-screeding floors, and replacing plumbing conduits.
  • Commercial Renovation: Adapting legacy warehouses into modern open-plan office spaces.
  • Building Extensions: Cantilever balcony additions and rooftop room constructions.
  • Structural Modifications: Safely removing load-bearing partitions with RSJ steel beam underpinning.
  • Repair & Maintenance: Structural crack injection, spall repair, and membrane re-waterproofing.

Division 07

Construction Management

Technical stewardship of the entire contracting lifecycle: rigorous Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material supply chain verification, site safety audits, and milestone timeline delivery.

Detailed Service Scope Includes:

  • Project Planning: Developing Work Breakdown Structures (WBS) and Critical Path Method (CPM) charts.
  • Cost Estimation: Granular rate analysis for civil materials, formwork rentals, and skilled labor.
  • Material Coordination: Just-in-time delivery for cement, steel, and aggregates to prevent site weathering.
  • Site Supervision: Resident civil engineers conducting daily checks on bar bending and cube tests.
  • Quality Inspection: Comprehensive pre-pour checklists and post-pour defect audits.
  • Project Scheduling: Weekly progress reporting against milestone schedules.
