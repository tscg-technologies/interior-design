Our Construction Process

A disciplined, milestone-driven civil engineering methodology ensuring safety, cost transparency, and structural durability at every phase.

Systematic Engineering Methodology

10 Defined Stages to Zero-Defect Construction

Civil construction demands systematic coordination between geotechnical studies, structural schematics, material procurement, concrete hydration curing, and trades synchronization. Here is how CIVIL CONTRACTOR executes work seamlessly from raw soil to final occupancy.

01

💬 Initial Consultation

Comprehensive introductory meeting to understand the client's functional space requirements, budget parameters, architectural goals, and anticipated project timeline. We align expectations regarding civil specifications and contracting models (labor contract, item-rate, or turnkey turnkey package).

02

📍 Site Visit & Geotechnical Assessment

On-site evaluation of the plot topography, road access for heavy transit mixers, groundwater levels, and boundary markers. We commission standard soil bore log tests to determine safe soil bearing capacity (SBC) and identify any subterranean rock or water tables.

03

📋 Requirement Analysis & Feasibility

Detailed scrutiny of local municipal building bylaws, setback rules, Floor Area Ratios (FAR), rainwater harvesting mandates, and environmental regulations. We ensure all planned dimensions and structural loads harmonize with municipal statutory codes.

04

📜 Planning & Technical Structural Design

Structural engineering design calculations using Finite Element Analysis software. Preparation of detailed foundation footings schedules, column coordinates, beam reinforcement layouts, slab thickness matrices, and architectural MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) civil sleeve drawings.

05

💰 Cost Estimation & Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Formulation of an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) detailing cubic meters of concrete, metric tons of TMT steel, square meters of shuttering, masonry square footage, and finishing materials. Clear payment milestones tied directly to verifiable site deliverables.

06

📅 Project Scheduling & Mobilization

Development of a comprehensive Critical Path Method (CPM) timeline. Mobilization of site offices, temporary electrical and water connections, barricading, delivery of formwork scaffolding, and heavy earthmoving equipment deployment.

07

⚒ Construction Execution (Substructure & Superstructure)

Bulk excavation, anti-termite soil barriers, Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) mud mats, raft or isolated footing casting, plinth tie-beams, followed by vertical column reinforcement, shuttering staging, monolithic beam and slab pours, and autoclave aerated concrete (AAC) or brick masonry infill.

08

👷 Continuous Site Supervision & Safety Checks

Full-time resident civil engineers verify bar-bending schedules (BBS), cover block placements, concrete slump measurements, cube sampling, and formwork de-shuttering timelines. Daily safety briefings and mandatory PPE enforcement for zero-accident site operation.

09

🧪 Multi-Stage Quality Inspection & Testing

Laboratory crushing tests for concrete cubes at 7 and 28 days to verify characteristic compressive strength. Flood testing for wet area waterproofing, plum line verification for vertical wall alignments, and electrical/plumbing pressure drop audits.

10

🎉 Final Handover & As-Built Documentation

Joint client walkthrough and rectification of any minor punch list items. Delivery of comprehensive As-Built civil drawings, electrical routing diagrams, warranty certificates for waterproofing, and formal handover of keys for occupancy.

Key Pillars

Execution Controls & Site Management

The five critical operational systems that keep our construction sites functioning smoothly.

🔁

Project Coordination

Harmonizing civil teams with architectural designers, HVAC technicians, electrical contractors, and structural consultants through structured weekly coordination meetings.

📦

Material Planning

Scientific staging of cement bags in dry weather-sealed godowns, covered sand pits to prevent silt contamination, and corrosion-inhibited rebar storage.

🔍

Quality Control

Three-tier quality audits: craftsman self-checks, site engineer verification sign-offs, and periodic independent third-party laboratory material assessments.

🛡

Site Safety

Zero-tolerance safety mandate: perimeter safety catch nets, fall arrest harness anchors, double handrails on open edges, and clear emergency muster points.

👷

Construction Supervision

Daily digital site logs capturing temperature, pour volumes, manpower counts, curing records, and photographic milestone logs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how civil contracting projects are managed, scheduled, and delivered.

An architect conceives the aesthetic, spatial layout, and functional envelope of a building. A civil contractor translates those drawings into structural reality—managing earthworks, concrete pouring, steel framing, heavy equipment, site safety, labor, and compliance with structural engineering codes.
Concrete quality is verified in real-time on site. A slump cone test is performed on each transit mixer batch to ensure proper workability. Simultaneously, standard 150mm test cubes are cast, labeled, cured on site, and crushed in calibrated compression testing machines at 7 days and 28 days to confirm design strength (e.g. M25 or M30).
We support three primary models: (1) Turnkey Contract (materials, equipment, and labor included from foundation to handover), (2) Item-Rate Contract (priced per unit of measured civil work based on a detailed BOQ), and (3) Pure Labor & Equipment Contracting (where the client procures major materials).
Prior to excavation, geotechnical soil bore tests identify major subsurface layers. If unexpected boulders, water tables, or loose soil strata are encountered, our structural engineering team immediately assesses the conditions, recalculates footing dimensions or recommends isolated rock trenching/piling without compromising safety.
Curing maintains adequate moisture within the concrete mass to allow the chemical reaction known as cement hydration to occur. Inadequate curing results in surface shrinkage cracking, reduced compressive strength, and high permeability that accelerates rebar corrosion. We enforce continuous 14 to 21-day curing regimes.
